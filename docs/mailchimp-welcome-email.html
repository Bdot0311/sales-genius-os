A note from Brandon

Start with a real reason to reach out

Hi there,
 
I'm Brandon, founder of OutReign. Thanks for being here. I started Field Notes to share something useful: practical ways to find the right prospects and start conversations without sounding like everyone else in their inbox.
 
The goal isn't more emails. It's a better reason to send the next one.
 
A useful idea in every edition
 
We'll break down prospect research, email writing and follow-ups into things you can try—not just theory. You'll get complete articles right here in your inbox, with examples and a quick tip you can put to work.
 
Expect guidance on checking research before using it, turning a real company detail into a natural opening, and reviewing AI-assisted drafts so the final message still sounds like you.
 
Start with one conversation
 
Pick one company you'd genuinely like to work with. Find one relevant fact from an original source. Then write a short question about it. That gives you a more thoughtful starting point than a generic pitch.
 
What's the hardest part of outreach for you right now? Finding the right people, knowing what to say, or getting a conversation started? Hit reply and tell me—your answer can help shape what we cover next.
 
If you'd like to put these ideas into practice, explore OutReign. Start with a prospect, review the research and draft, and approve only the outreach you're happy to send.
 
Thanks for giving Field Notes a place in your inbox.
Brandon
Founder, OutReign
 
 

💡 Tip of the week


Before writing your first email, find one specific company detail and verify it at the original source. Use that detail to ask a short, relevant question—not to assume a problem.


Start small: research one prospect and review one draft before sending.
